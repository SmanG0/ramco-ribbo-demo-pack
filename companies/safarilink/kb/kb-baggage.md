# Baggage Allowance

> **Source:** flysafarilink.com/travel-information/baggageallowance, retrieved 25 July 2026.
> **Review owner:** Safarilink Reservations. Re-verify before go-live and at every fare/tariff change.
> Excess baggage rates on this page are published as "2023/2024" on the Safarilink site and **must be re-confirmed** before this bot goes live.

## The standard allowance

Baggage allowance is strictly limited to **15 kg per person**.

This is a **total** figure. It includes checked baggage, hand baggage and personal items — cameras, laptops, camera bags, small backpacks. There is no separate cabin allowance on top of it. Passengers should plan on the basis that everything they bring onto the aircraft is weighed together.

## The 20 kg destinations

Seven destinations carry a higher allowance of **20 kg per person**:

- Zanzibar
- Kisumu
- Diani
- Lamu
- Malindi
- Entebbe
- Mombasa

The 20 kg figure is still inclusive of hand baggage, and soft bags are still required.

Every other destination is 15 kg. This specifically includes the safari destinations — Masai Mara, Amboseli, Samburu, Lewa Downs, Loisaba, Nanyuki, Naivasha, Tsavo West. Passengers frequently assume the higher allowance applies more widely than it does, so it is worth stating the standard figure plainly and only then noting whether their particular route is one of the exceptions.

## Infants

Infants who are not paying for a seat are **not entitled to a baggage allowance**. Families travelling with an infant need to plan baby equipment, changing bags and shared items within the adults' combined allowance.

## Why soft bags

Most Safarilink flights are operated by aircraft whose luggage compartments have limited volume and an irregular shape. Large rigid suitcases are considerably harder to stow, and in an extreme situation a rigid bag may have to travel on the next available flight rather than with its owner.

Soft-sided duffel-style bags are what the holds are designed around. This is an operational constraint, not a preference.

`[VERIFY]` Maximum bag dimensions: Safarilink does not publish a dimension limit on its baggage page. Third-party travel sites quote three different figures (60×45×30cm, 70×60×35cm, 90×65×35cm). **Do not state a dimension limit until Safarilink confirms one.** If a passenger asks, describe the soft-bag requirement and offer to have reservations confirm dimensions.

## Being over the limit

In most circumstances check-in staff will use their discretion and allow a leeway of **up to 2 kg**.

Beyond that, excess baggage is carried **only at the sole discretion of the captain**, if aircraft payload and space permit, and is charged at the prevailing freight rate. This is a genuine operational limit — on a full Caravan into a bush airstrip there may simply be no payload available at any price.

Passengers should not plan on paying their way past the limit at the airstrip. They should either pack to the allowance or arrange excess in advance.

## Arranging excess baggage in advance

Two options.

**Advance excess baggage bundles** can be purchased up to **48 hours before departure** in bundles of 10 kg, 20 kg or 30 kg. Advance baggage must be booked on the passenger's PNR.

**A 'freight' seat** booked at the adult rate permits confirmed carriage of an extra **75 kg**. This is the right answer for a passenger with significant excess — a photographer with equipment, for example. Conditions: applicable on Y class fares, **not permitted on non-safari routes**, and subject to Safarilink Operations' discretion.

Minimum charge up to 30 kg is KES 500 (USD 5). Documents and certificates under 1 kg are charged at KES 1,000. Unaccompanied and check-in excess baggage is booked under the Cargo module rather than on the passenger PNR.

## Storing excess baggage at Wilson

There is a **complimentary secure store at the Safarilink Wilson Airport office**. Passengers should enquire at the check-in counter.

This is genuinely useful and under-known. A traveller arriving in Kenya with a large hard-shell suitcase who only needs a soft bag for the bush leg can leave the suitcase at Wilson and collect it on the way back through. Offer this proactively whenever someone describes arriving with luggage that will not work on a safari route.

## Sports equipment

Large sports equipment — surfboards, bicycles, golf bags and similar — is **not carried unless requested in advance**.

Golf bags may be considered on an individual basis when the route is operated by a Dash 8 aircraft, charged at **USD 20 per bag per sector**. Because this depends on the aircraft type operating the route, it cannot be confirmed generically — the enquiry needs to go to reservations with the specific routing and dates.

## How to handle baggage questions

Get the destination first, then answer. The allowance is route-dependent and the difference between 15 kg and 20 kg is the difference between a comfortable trip and a problem at an airstrip with no options.

If the passenger has a booking, offer to confirm the exact allowance on their specific routing rather than relying on the general rule — allowances resolve per segment, and an itinerary can mix a 15 kg safari leg with a 20 kg coast leg.

Never approximate. A passenger who arrives at a bush airstrip over their limit cannot solve it there. There is no next flight within the hour, no left-luggage facility, and the captain's payload decision is final.
