## Not JKIA

Safarilink flights from Nairobi depart from **Wilson Airport (WIL)**, not Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA/NBO).

This is the most consequential piece of information in this document. A passenger who goes to the wrong airport misses their flight, and on safari routes there may not be another one that day. Whenever a passenger mentions arriving in Nairobi, connecting from an international flight, or being "at the airport", confirm which airport they mean.

## Where it is

Wilson Airport is on Langata Road, roughly 6 km from Nairobi city centre and approximately 17 km from JKIA.

The Safarilink office is at **Phoenix House, Wilson Airport**.

## Transfer from JKIA

`[VERIFY — confirm recommended minimum connection time with Safarilink Reservations]`

Nairobi traffic is highly variable and the JKIA–Wilson transfer can take substantially longer than the distance suggests, particularly at peak times. Passengers connecting from an international arrival should allow generous time and should not plan a tight same-day connection without confirming it with reservations.

Where a passenger holds a Kenya Airways codeshare booking, see `kb-codeshare-kq.md` for connection guidance.

## The terminal

Wilson does not operate like a large airport. There is no single central departures terminal — each airline operates from its own check-in building across the airport compound. Passengers should look for the **Safarilink** check-in building specifically, and allow time to find it on a first visit.

## Luggage storage

There is a **complimentary secure luggage store at the Safarilink Wilson Airport office**. Passengers should enquire at the check-in counter.

This is the answer for anyone arriving in Kenya with a hard-shell suitcase who only needs a soft bag for a safari leg. See `kb-baggage.md`.
