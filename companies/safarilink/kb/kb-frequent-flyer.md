# Frequent Flyer Programme

> **Source:** flysafarilink.com/about-us/frequentflyer
> `[VERIFY — the full programme rules, tier structure, earning rates and redemption mechanics must be retrieved from the page above before go-live. Do not answer earning or redemption questions from this document until populated.]`

## What is known

Safarilink operates a frequent flyer programme. Members can earn miles on flights and buy award tickets. Sign-up is available through the Safarilink website and includes:

- Personalised offers
- Saved details for faster booking
- Mile earning on every trip
- Award ticket purchase

## Structure to populate

1. **Enrolment** — who is eligible, how to join, cost
2. **Earning** — rate per sector or per fare, whether it varies by fare class, whether codeshare sectors earn
3. **Tiers** — names, qualification thresholds, benefits at each tier, qualification period
4. **Redemption** — what miles buy, award availability rules, taxes and charges payable on award tickets
5. **Expiry** — mile validity period and any activity requirement
6. **Lounge access** — whether any tier confers Executive Safari Lounge access
7. **Family or corporate pooling** — if offered

## Handling these questions until populated

Confirm the programme exists, describe the general benefits above, direct the member to flysafarilink.com/about-us/frequentflyer or their account login, and offer a handoff for anything specific about their own balance or a redemption.

**Never state a mile balance, an earning figure, a tier status or an award price.** Balances and status are per-member live data and belong behind a tool, not in this document. Until that tool exists, these questions go to a human.
