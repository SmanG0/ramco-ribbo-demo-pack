## Why punctuality matters more here

Safarilink operates light aircraft on tight rotations, often as circuits calling at several airstrips. Weight and balance calculations are done per flight based on actual passenger and baggage weights, and the aircraft cannot wait.

Arriving early is not a courtesy — it is what allows baggage to be weighed, loaded and balanced correctly.

## Baggage at check-in

All baggage is weighed, including hand baggage and camera equipment, because the allowance is a combined total. See `kb-baggage.md`.

Check-in staff will use discretion for a leeway of up to 2 kg. Beyond that, excess is subject to the captain's discretion on payload and space, and is charged at the prevailing freight rate.

Excess baggage arranged in advance — bundles or a freight seat — should be booked before arriving at the airport. Excess presented at check-in within 6 hours of departure is charged at a higher rate and is subject to space.

## Boarding

Safarilink operates a dedicated boarding lounge at Wilson Airport.

On light aircraft, seating may be allocated by the crew for weight and balance reasons rather than by seat number. Passengers should not expect to choose their seat on Caravan operations.
