## Airstrips vs airports

This is the single most important thing to establish on a destination enquiry. "Masai Mara" is not one place — it is a group of airstrips serving different conservancies, and the correct airstrip depends entirely on which camp or lodge the passenger is staying at.

**Always ask which camp or lodge they are booked into, then work backwards to the airstrip.** A passenger who lands at the wrong Mara airstrip may be an hour or more of game drive away from their camp, and transfers are arranged by the camp rather than the airline.

If a passenger does not know their airstrip, the camp will. Direct them to ask their lodge or tour operator, and offer to have reservations confirm once they know.

## What to expect at a bush airstrip

Bush airstrips are not airports. There is typically a strip, a windsock, a shade structure and little else. There is no terminal building, no baggage system, no shops and no left luggage. Camps send vehicles to meet arriving passengers.

Flights on safari routes often operate as a circuit, calling at more than one airstrip in sequence. This means the flight a passenger is on may make an intermediate landing before reaching their destination — this is normal and not a delay.

Passengers should expect to be weighed along with their baggage on light aircraft operations, and seating may be allocated by the crew for weight and balance reasons rather than by seat number.

## Coast and regional destinations

Coast and regional destinations operate into established airports with terminal facilities. These are also the routes that carry the higher 20 kg baggage allowance — Diani, Mombasa, Malindi, Lamu, Kisumu, Zanzibar and Entebbe.

## Destinations we do not serve

If a passenger asks about somewhere outside the network, say so plainly and offer the alternatives: a private charter enquiry, or the Kenya Airways codeshare where it applies to their routing.

Never invent a route, a connection or a schedule. An itinerary built on a flight that does not exist causes real harm to a traveller's trip.
