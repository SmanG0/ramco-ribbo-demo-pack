# Charters

> **Source:** flysafarilink.com/travel-information/charters
> `[VERIFY — confirm charter aircraft availability, minimum notice and the enquiry-to-quote process with Safarilink Charters before go-live]`

## What charter is

A private charter is an aircraft booked for the exclusive use of one party, flying to a schedule the client sets rather than the published timetable. Safarilink operates charters alongside its scheduled network.

Charter is the right answer when a passenger needs:

- A destination outside the scheduled network
- A departure time the timetable does not offer
- A group large enough to fill an aircraft
- Flexibility to change timings
- A routing that scheduled services would make impractical

## What the bot does with charter enquiries

Charter pricing depends on aircraft type, routing, block time, positioning, crew duty and turnaround. It cannot be quoted from a rate card and the bot must never attempt a figure.

The bot's job is to **capture a complete enquiry** and route it to the charter team. Gather:

1. Departure point and destination or destinations
2. Preferred date or dates, and whether they are flexible
3. Number of passengers
4. Whether it is one way or return, and if return, the intended layover
5. Approximate total baggage
6. Any special requirements — medical, mobility, equipment, livestock, freight
7. Contact name, telephone and email

Then tell them the charter team will come back with options and pricing, and give a response window.

## What the bot does not do

- Quote a charter price, or a range, or a comparison to a scheduled fare
- Confirm aircraft availability
- Commit to a departure time
- Advise on whether a bush airstrip can accept a given aircraft type

All of these depend on operational assessment. Capture the enquiry, hand it over.

## Baggage on charter

Charter baggage allowances are set per charter based on aircraft payload and routing, and differ from the scheduled 15 kg and 20 kg allowances. Do not apply scheduled allowances to a charter enquiry.

## Freight

Passengers with significant excess baggage on a **scheduled** flight should be pointed at the freight seat option in `kb-baggage.md` rather than at charter — a freight seat at the adult rate carries an extra 75 kg and is far cheaper than chartering. Freight seats apply on Y class fares, on safari routes only, at Safarilink Operations' discretion.
