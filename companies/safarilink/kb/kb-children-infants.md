# Children and Infants

> `[VERIFY — ALL age thresholds, fare rules and seat requirements in this document must be confirmed with Safarilink Reservations before go-live.]`

## What is known from primary sources

**Infant baggage.** Infants who are not paying for a seat are **not entitled to a baggage allowance**. Source: flysafarilink.com baggage page.

This is the single most practically important fact in this document. Families travelling with a baby must plan nappies, formula, changing bags and equipment within the adults' combined 15 kg or 20 kg. On a safari route with two adults and an infant that is 30 kg total for the whole family including all hand baggage and camera equipment. Say this early and clearly — it is a genuine planning constraint and families are routinely caught by it.

**Multiple infants.** The Safarilink booking system limits the number of infants per booking to a maximum of 6.

**Unaccompanied minors.** Safarilink's booking system **does not permit unaccompanied minors to be booked online**. The system message directs the customer to contact the airline directly.

## Handling unaccompanied minors

**Always hand off.** Do not attempt to advise on whether a child can travel alone, at what age, under what supervision, or with what documentation.

An unaccompanied minor booking involves age thresholds, handover procedures at both ends, documentation, and a duty of care that Safarilink accepts. It cannot be arranged through a chatbot and the airline's own system explicitly routes it to a human. Say warmly that this needs to be arranged directly with the team, and connect them.

## Light aircraft considerations

Passengers and baggage are weighed for weight and balance on light aircraft operations, and crew allocate seating accordingly. Families should not expect to choose seats together on Caravan operations, though crews will accommodate where balance permits.

Flights are short — typically 45 to 90 minutes — with no meal service. Families should bring what a child needs, remembering it counts within the baggage allowance.
