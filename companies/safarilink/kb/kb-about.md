# About Safarilink

> **Source:** flysafarilink.com, retrieved 25 July 2026.

## Who we are

Safarilink Aviation Limited is Kenya's premier safari airline, operating a network of connecting domestic scheduled services to destinations across Kenya, Uganda and northern Tanzania including Zanzibar Island.

The airline marked its **20th anniversary** in 2024, having been founded in 2004. It is part of Ramco Oritsu, the services and trading arm of the Ramco Group of Companies.

## Base

Safarilink operates from **Wilson Airport, Nairobi** — not Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. The office is at Phoenix House, Wilson Airport.

This distinction matters constantly. Passengers connecting from an international arrival at JKIA must transfer across Nairobi to Wilson, and the two airports are frequently confused. Always confirm which airport a passenger means.

## Safety

Safarilink holds **IATA Standard Safety Assessment (ISSA) certification**. ISSA is the IATA safety assessment programme designed for operators of aircraft below the IOSA size threshold, and certification follows an independent audit against international standards.

The fleet comprises turboprop aircraft suited to both bush airstrip and regional operations. Aircraft type matters to passengers for two reasons: cabin size and baggage handling. Golf bags, for example, may be considered only on the larger Dash 8 aircraft.

In June 2025 Safarilink signed a letter of intent with Aura Aero regarding the ERA aircraft, indicating a fleet renewal programme.

## Sustainability

Safarilink supports a range of conservation and community programmes including the Lewa Wildlife Conservancy, Mt Kenya Trust, Colobus Conservation, WildlifeDirect's 'Hands Off Our Elephants' campaign, the annual Aitong Medical Camp in the Masai Mara, the Lion Kids Camp, and a school partnership programme. The airline has been recognised at the Eco Warrior Awards.

## What we do not offer

There is no in-flight WiFi. Flights are short — typically 45 to 90 minutes — with no meal service or in-flight entertainment.
