# Delivery

> **Source:** sai-office.com/kenya — "Efficient Delivery 48 Hours". Retrieved 25 July 2026.
> `[VERIFY]` Delivery zones, upcountry coverage, order cut-off times, charges and what the 48-hour commitment covers all require confirmation from Sai Office before go-live.

## The commitment

Sai Office publishes a delivery commitment of **48 hours**.

This is a published promise and the bot may state it confidently. It is also one of the more useful things a bot can do in this business — a customer placing a reorder wants to know when it lands, and being able to say "that'll be with you within 48 hours" without anyone having to check is real value.

`[VERIFY]` Confirm precisely what the 48 hours covers before the bot applies it broadly:

1. Does it apply to **stock items only**, or to everything?
2. Is it **48 working hours** or 48 calendar hours?
3. Does it run from **order placement** or from order confirmation and payment?
4. Does it cover **Nairobi only**, or upcountry, or all four countries?
5. Does it apply to **furniture, solar and cooling** — which typically involve lead times and installation — or only to consumables and stock IT?

Until these are answered, the bot should state the 48-hour commitment as Sai Office's standard delivery service and offer to confirm the specific date for the customer's order, rather than promising 48 hours on a five-metre boardroom table.

## Order cut-off

`[VERIFY]` Obtain the daily cut-off time for same-day processing. This is one of the most frequently asked delivery questions and the bot cannot answer it until confirmed.

## Cross-border

Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda operate as **separate legal entities**. A Kenyan order does not ship from Tanzania and vice versa. If a customer asks about delivery to another country, route them to that country's entity — the bot for that tenant, or the contact details for that market.

Do not treat the four countries as one delivery network.

## Lines with genuine lead times

Furniture, solar and cooling solutions typically involve survey, specification, ordering and installation rather than picking from stock. Do not apply the 48-hour commitment to these. Capture the requirement and let a salesperson give a realistic timeline.

## Under a lease

Consumables supplied under an Office Technologies lease follow the lease's own arrangements rather than standard order delivery. See `kb-leasing.md`.

## What the bot should do

For a stock consumable order from an account customer: confirm the item, confirm stock, state the expected delivery, log the order.

For anything involving installation, survey or non-stock supply: do not state a delivery date. Capture the requirement and hand off.

If the customer asks something in this document marked `[VERIFY]` and it has not yet been populated, say that delivery timing depends on the item and location, and offer to have someone confirm. Do not guess a cut-off time or a zone charge.
