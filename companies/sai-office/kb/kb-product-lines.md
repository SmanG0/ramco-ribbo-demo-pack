# Product and Service Lines

> **Source:** sai-office.com/kenya/products-services. Retrieved 25 July 2026.
> **No prices in this document.** Sai Office does not publish prices. All pricing comes from a tool for account customers, or from a captured quote request for everyone else.

## The seven lines

Sai Office is not a single-category supplier. Establishing which line an enquiry belongs to is the first thing to do on any conversation, because the follow-up questions differ completely.

### 1. I.T Solutions & Office Automation
Computing technology for routine business operations. Desktops, laptops, servers, printers, scanners, networking, power protection and media.

Brands include Dell, HP, Lenovo, Canon, Epson, Brother, D-Link, APC, Eaton, Tripp Lite, Schneider, Blue Edge.

### 2. Stationery
One-stop supply of stationery goods, through Sai Office's own brands **OfficePoint** and **Veda**, plus **Skoolpoint** for education.

Sai Office is the **authorised distributor for Crayola and UHU in East Africa**. Other represented brands include Pritt, Maped, Helix Oxford, Rexel, Fellowes, Kangaro and Ortea.

### 3. Office Furniture
Modern office furniture and facilities. OfficePoint is the in-house furniture brand.

`[VERIFY]` Confirm whether furniture involves site survey, space planning or installation services, and typical lead times, which differ from stock consumables.

### 4. Solar
Solar equipment supply, positioned around reducing energy bills.

`[VERIFY]` Confirm scope — whether Sai Office supplies equipment only or also designs and installs systems, and whether there is a site assessment process. This materially changes how the bot should handle a solar enquiry.

### 5. Cooling Solutions
Air conditioners, commercial outdoor units and cassette air conditioners. Sai Office is described as an official distributor in this category.

`[VERIFY]` Confirm the AC brands represented, and whether installation and servicing are provided.

### 6. Leasing
Leasing of printers and photocopiers through **Office Technologies Limited**, together with digital document storage. See `kb-leasing.md`.

### 7. Services
The **Epson and APC authorised service centre**, in-warranty and out-of-warranty, with qualified engineers and a stocked spare parts division. See `kb-service-centre.md`.

## Routing an enquiry

Work out the line before gathering detail. Signals:

| Customer says | Line |
|---|---|
| toner, cartridge, paper, pens, files, ink | Stationery / IT consumables |
| laptop, desktop, server, UPS, router, printer to buy | IT Solutions |
| desks, chairs, workstations, reception, boardroom | Office Furniture |
| solar, panels, inverter, battery, power bills | Solar |
| air conditioning, AC, cooling, cassette unit | Cooling Solutions |
| lease, rental, contract, meter reading, copier volume | Leasing |
| repair, broken, not working, warranty, service, spare part | Services |

Where an enquiry spans lines — a new office needing furniture, IT and AC — capture it as a single project enquiry and hand off to a salesperson rather than trying to quote piecemeal.

## Catalogues

Product catalogues are published at sai-office.com/kenya/product-catalogues. Direct customers there for browsing, and offer to check stock or pricing on anything specific.

## What the bot does not do

Sai Office does not publish prices and the site uses a Product Enquiry form rather than a shopping cart. So:

- For a **recognised account customer**, quote their contract price from a tool.
- For **anyone else**, do not quote or estimate. Capture a quote request with the specification and contact details, and tell them a salesperson will come back with pricing.

Never invent a price, a range, or a comparison. Never suggest what something "usually costs."
