> **Source:** sai-office.com/kenya brand wall and product pages; Africa Business Directory listing. Retrieved 25 July 2026.

## Own brands

Sai Office develops and owns:

- **OfficePoint** — office furniture and stationery
- **Veda** — stationery
- **Skoolpoint** — education and school stationery

These matter commercially. Where a customer is price-sensitive, own-brand is often the right recommendation, and Sai Office controls availability and margin on them. The bot may mention that own-brand alternatives exist, but must not compare prices — see `kb-accounts-and-ordering.md`.

## Appointed distributorships

Sai Office is cited as the appointed distributor in East Africa for:

- **Crayola**
- **UHU**

And as appointed **service centre** for:

- **Epson**
- **APC**

See `kb-service-centre.md`. The service centre appointment is a genuine differentiator — it means local diagnosis, local parts and local repair rather than shipping equipment abroad.

## Warranty by brand

## Compatibility questions

Customers frequently ask which consumable fits which device — "what toner does my Epson WF-7840 take."

`[VERIFY]` Whether a device-to-consumable compatibility dataset exists and can be exposed to a tool. This is a high-frequency question and answering it well would be genuinely valuable.

**Until such a tool exists the bot must not answer these questions specifically.** Give general guidance on locating a model number, point at the published product catalogues, and offer to have someone confirm the exact part. A wrong cartridge recommendation results in a return, a credit note and an annoyed customer.
