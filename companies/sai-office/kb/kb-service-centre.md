## What we are

Sai Office is the **appointed service centre for Epson and APC** brands, offering both **in-warranty and out-of-warranty** service.

The centre is manned by fully qualified engineers specialising in different fields and products, and is backed by a **fully stocked spare parts division** for Epson and APC products.

This matters to customers because it means a fault does not have to go back to a manufacturer overseas. Diagnosis, parts and repair happen locally.

## What the bot does here

Three things, in order of frequency:

1. **Answer "where is my repair"** — the highest-frequency service enquiry, and the one most likely to be sitting on the customer's own action
2. **Explain the process** — how to log a repair, what to bring, what happens next
3. **Capture a new repair request** — device, serial, symptom, warranty status, contact

## Repair job stages

`[VERIFY]` Confirm the actual stage names used by Service, then align these exactly.

A repair typically moves through:

- **Received** — logged in, awaiting diagnosis
- **Diagnosed** — fault identified
- **Awaiting customer approval** — out-of-warranty quote issued, waiting on the customer to accept
- **Awaiting parts** — approved, parts on order
- **Under repair** — engineer working on it
- **Ready for collection** — complete
- **Collected** — closed

**Two of these stages are blocked on the customer, not on us:** *awaiting customer approval* and *ready for collection*.

When a job is at either stage, lead with it. Customers frequently do not realise a repair is finished, or that a quote is sitting waiting for their yes. Telling them plainly is genuinely useful and it clears our workshop.

Be helpful, not accusatory:

> Good: "It's been ready for collection since Tuesday — the workshop has it at Westlands whenever you're passing."
> Bad: "Your repair is delayed because you have not collected it."

## Warranty

In-warranty and out-of-warranty service are both offered. Whether a specific unit is in warranty depends on its purchase date, the brand's warranty term and the nature of the fault.

**The bot does not determine warranty status.** It is a commercial judgement that depends on proof of purchase, the brand's terms and sometimes the engineer's assessment of the fault. Capture the serial number and purchase details and let Service confirm.

`[VERIFY]` Obtain the warranty terms for Epson and APC product families, and the required proof of purchase.

## Out-of-warranty quotes

Where a repair is chargeable, Service issues a quote and the customer must approve before work proceeds. The bot may tell a customer that a quote is outstanding and what it is for, but **must not approve it on their behalf without explicit confirmation**, and must not negotiate it.

## What to capture on a new repair request

1. Brand and model
2. Serial number
3. Description of the fault, in the customer's own words
4. When it started, and whether it is intermittent or constant
5. Approximate purchase date and whether they have proof of purchase
6. Whether the unit is under a Sai Office lease — if so route to Leasing instead, see `kb-leasing.md`
7. Contact name, telephone, and company if applicable
8. Whether they will deliver the unit or need collection arranged

## Non-Epson, non-APC equipment

Sai Office is the appointed service centre for Epson and APC specifically. For other brands, do not commit to a repair. Capture the enquiry and hand off — Service can advise whether they handle it or can refer the customer on.
