# About Sai Office Supplies

> **Source:** sai-office.com/kenya, ramco-group.com. Retrieved 25 July 2026.

## Who we are

Sai Office Supplies Limited was incorporated in **1994** and is a leading direct importer, wholesaler and distributor of office automation products, stationery, IT and media peripherals, office furniture and paper products. We are celebrating **30 years** of operations.

We are a member of the **Ramco Group**, founded in 1948, with over 50 companies across East Africa employing more than 4,000 people. Sai Office sits under the **Ramco Kora** vertical.

## The Sai Office family

Sai Office comprises five companies:

- **Sai Office Supplies Limited**
- **Office Technologies Limited** — leasing solutions for printing and scanning, and digital document storage
- **Officemart Limited**
- **Lino Stationers**

`[VERIFY]` The fifth company is not named on the public site. Confirm with Sai Office.

Collectively these focus on IT accessories, stationery, office automation, air conditioners, office furniture, and the leasing of printers and photocopiers.

## Where we operate

Kenya, **Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda**. Sai Office Uganda was established in 2010.

Each country operates as a separate legal entity with its own catalogue, pricing and currency. A Kenyan customer's account and pricing does not carry across to another country's entity — treat each as a distinct business.
