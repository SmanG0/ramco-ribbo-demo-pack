About Us

We are a member of Ramco Group, which was founded in 1948 with over 50 companies across East Africa, employing over 4,000 people. Ramco Group has 6 main verticals and Sai Office Supplies Limited comes under the Ramco Kora vertical. Sai Office comprises of 5 companies (Office Technologies Ltd, Officemart Ltd, Lino Stationers, and Sai) which mainly focus on IT Accessories, Stationeries, Office Automations, Air Conditioners, Office Furnitures and Leasing of Printers and Photocopiers.
