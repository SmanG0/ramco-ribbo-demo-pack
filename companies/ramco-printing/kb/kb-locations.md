## Why the bot must get this right

**Collection location depends on which division holds the job.**

A customer collecting business cards goes to Industrial Area. A customer collecting a pull-up banner, a diary order or packaging goes to Mombasa Road. These are on opposite sides of Nairobi, and sending someone to the wrong one wastes their morning and looks careless.

**Always state the collection site by name when confirming a job is ready**, and state which division it is with, so the customer can sanity-check it themselves.

If a customer has jobs with more than one division, say so explicitly — they may need two collections, or ask for consolidation. `[VERIFY]` whether inter-site consolidation is offered.

## Collection

## Delivery

`[VERIFY]` Obtain:

1. Delivery zones — Nairobi, upcountry, regional
2. Charges and whether they vary by zone or job size
3. Typical delivery lead time after dispatch
4. Whether part-delivery is offered on large runs
5. Whether the customer must be present, and what happens if nobody can receive

Do not state a delivery date. The bot may report that a job has been **dispatched** and, once verified, roughly when to expect it. Committing to a delivery date is the planner's job.

## Showroom

The contemporary showroom is at the **Dunga Road unit** (UNO site).

Use it. Where a customer cannot decide on stock, finish, binding or a promotional item, suggest visiting the showroom to see samples. It resolves specification questions the bot cannot, and it gets them in front of a person.

`[VERIFY]` Confirm showroom opening hours and whether an appointment is preferred.
