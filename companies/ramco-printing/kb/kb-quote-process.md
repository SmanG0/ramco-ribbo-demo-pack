# Quoting and Specification

> `[VERIFY]` Quote turnaround times and which product types can be priced from a rate card require confirmation from a Ramco Printing estimator before go-live.
> Read `kb-divisions.md` first. Nothing here happens until the division is known.

## Why print cannot be looked up

A print job is **configured, not selected**. There is no catalogue price for "5,000 notebooks" because the answer depends on size, cover, stock, colours, finishing, binding and quantity break — and on press time, imposition and wastage, which the customer cannot see.

So the bot's job is not to find a price. It is to **elicit a complete, valid specification** and get it to an estimator in a state they can price without going back to the customer.

That is the real value here. Estimators lose hours chasing customers for missing information. A bot that captures a complete brief on first contact, at any hour, removes that entirely.

## What to gather

Only what applies to the product. Ask **two or three things at a time** — not one at a time, which is tedious, and not all at once, which is overwhelming.

1. **Product type** — what is it
2. **Quantity** — and whether they want alternative quantity breaks priced
3. **Finished size**
4. **Stock** — paper or board, weight, finish
5. **Colours** — full colour both sides, one colour, and so on
6. **Finishing** — lamination, binding, die-cutting, foiling
7. **Deadline** — when they need it in hand
8. **Delivery or collection** — and to where

## Translate their language

Customers do not speak in print terms and should not have to. Translate, then confirm back in plain words.

| They say | Record | Confirm back as |
|---|---|---|
| "shiny cover" | gloss lamination | "gloss finish on the cover" |
| "thick paper" | ask for a gsm range or offer options | "a heavier stock — I'll get options priced" |
| "double sided colour" | 4/4 | "full colour both sides" |
| "like a proper book" | perfect bound | "perfect bound, like a paperback" |
| "spiral" | wiro or spiral bound | "spiral bound" |

**Never invent a specification value the customer did not give.** If they do not know, record it as unspecified and let the estimator advise. An unspecified field is fine; a wrong assumed one produces a quote for the wrong job.

## Indicative pricing — the hard limit

The bot may give an **indicative range** for standard, rate-carded items only. When it does, it must always:

- Call it indicative
- Say an estimator will confirm
- State what it excludes — typically artwork origination and delivery

For **anything outside the rate card**, the bot gives **no number at all.** Not a guess, not "probably around", not a comparison to a similar job, not a per-unit figure.

Print estimating depends on paper cost, press time, imposition and wastage. A wrong number costs real money and makes Ramco Printing look amateur to its own customer. When in doubt: no number, full spec captured, estimator informed.

`[VERIFY]` Confirm with an estimator which product types may be quoted indicatively and which must always be estimated manually.

## Deadlines

Always ask when they need it. Compare against typical turnaround plus any lead time on stock or finishing.

- **Comfortable** — say so and proceed
- **Tight** — say so plainly, flag the request as urgent, and proceed. Do not refuse and do not pretend it is comfortable
- **Not achievable** — say that too, and offer what is: a reduced quantity, a simpler finish, a part delivery of the urgent portion first

Offering an alternative is far more useful than a flat no. A customer with a conference on Friday would rather have 2,000 notebooks on time than 5,000 late.

## Multi-division and project work

Where an enquiry spans divisions — a brand rollout needing signage, stationery and packaging — do not split it into separate quote requests. Capture it once as a project enquiry and hand off to a salesperson who can coordinate.

## Undecided customers

Where a customer cannot decide on stock, finish or binding, suggest the **showroom at the Dunga Road unit**. Seeing samples resolves it faster than any description, and it puts them in front of a person. See `kb-locations.md`.

## What the bot does not do

- Price non-standard work
- Commit to a delivery date — it states typical turnaround and flags urgency
- Accept artwork through this channel
- Negotiate price or offer discounts
- Discuss another customer's quotes, jobs or artwork
