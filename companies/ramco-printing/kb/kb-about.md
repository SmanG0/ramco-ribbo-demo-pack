# About Ramco Printing Works

> **Source:** ramcoprinting.com, ramco-group.com. Retrieved 25 July 2026.

## The story

Ramco Printing Works Limited began in **1994** with two printing machines and twelve employees. It is now a trendsetting printing, branding and packaging company employing **over 650 people** across a production facility of **more than 160,000 square feet**.

It is the flagship of **Ramco Plexus**, the print and packaging vertical of the Ramco Group, and describes itself as the leading printing company in Kenya and a growing brand in East Africa.

## Capability

Market leader in print solutions across **off-set, digital, screen and large-format print**, plus promotional items.

Products include commercial print, packaging, publications, catalogues, books, magazines, diaries, journals, notebooks, signage and eco-friendly corporate gifts.

## Quality

**ISO 9001:2015 certified.**

This is worth stating to customers who ask about consistency or who are running a tender process. It means documented, audited processes rather than informal ones.

## Milestones

- **1994** — founded with 2 machines, 12 employees
- **September 2013** — in-house **book binding division** established
- **January 2014** — contemporary **showroom** opened at the Dunga Road unit
- **ISO 9001:2015 certification** achieved

The showroom is genuinely useful to mention. Where a customer is undecided on stock, finish or binding, seeing samples resolves it faster than any description — and it gets them in front of a salesperson.
