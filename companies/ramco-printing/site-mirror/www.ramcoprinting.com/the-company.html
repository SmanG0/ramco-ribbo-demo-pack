The Company

At Ramco Printing, it is our business to understand and facilitate all your print requirements. Our years of professional accomplishments make us the leading printing company in Kenya and a growing brand in East Africa.

 

Ours is truly a remarkable evolutionary story. From our humble beginning in 1994 with only 2 printing machines and a work force of merely 12 employees, our continuous focus on exceeding print industry expectations has seen Ramco Printing Works grow. We are now a trendsetting printing, branding and packaging company, encompassing a professional and dedicated staff of over 650 employees. With more than 160,000 square feet production facility, high print quality, printing speed and on-schedule delivery that bespeak reliability have endeared Ramco Printing to its customers.

 

 

We have now formed four divisions to be able to service the growing needs of our clients; UNO deals with digital & commercial print, DUO offers packaging & publishing printing, HEX manufactures diaries, journals & notebooks, and IX supplies branding, signage & promo items. Each of these four operate as separate units but form a synergy and has poised Ramco Printing to be a one-stop-shop for all print needs.


As a member of the Ramco Group, we are also able to offer additional services in the related industries of large format printing, flexible packaging, polythene and corrugated cartons. We continuously strive to meet the demands of an ever-increasing client base. We are not simply a printing company; we are in the business of printing.
