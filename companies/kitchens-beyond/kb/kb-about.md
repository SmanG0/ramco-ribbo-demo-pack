# About Kitchens & Beyond

> **Source:** kitchensandbeyond.co.ke, ramco-group.com/our-companies/kitchens-beyond, company LinkedIn. Retrieved 25 July 2026.

## Who we are

Kitchens & Beyond is Kenya's premier one-stop shop for luxury interior solutions — kitchens, wardrobes, sanitaryware, tiles, vanities and 3D interior design. **Over 13 years** of trading.

Part of the **Ramco Group**, under the **Ekon** building materials and supplies vertical. Ramco Group was founded in 1948 and today comprises over 50 companies across East Africa.

## The story

The company began with a single ambition: to introduce **modular kitchens** to the Kenyan market, partnering with the Italian brand **Snaidero** to bring high-quality customisable kitchen solutions.

That success prompted a broader vision — to become a genuine one-stop shop for interior solutions. The range expanded into sanitaryware, wardrobes, tiles, vanities, built-in appliances and sinks and faucets, each brand selected for quality and craftsmanship.

## Recognition

**Superbrands status, awarded 2021–22 by public vote.** This matters as a credential because it was voted rather than judged — it reflects market reputation rather than an industry panel.

## The showroom

A one-of-a-kind lifestyle showroom at **The Promenade, General Mathenge Drive, Nairobi**.

The showroom is central to how this business works. Kitchens, wardrobes and sanitaryware are tactile, considered purchases — nobody commits to an Italian kitchen from a photograph. **Getting a qualified client into the showroom, with a brief already prepared, is the single most valuable thing this bot can do.** See `kb-showroom-visits.md`.

## What makes us different

**Quality floor.** No product in the range carries a warranty of less than **ten years**. See `kb-warranty.md`.

**In-house design.** Interior design services and **3D visualisation**, so clients can see a space before committing. See `kb-design-service.md`.

**Honesty about scope.** Where Kitchens & Beyond does not stock what a client needs, the stated practice is to recommend providers who do. That is a genuine differentiator and the bot should reflect it — being helpful beyond the catalogue builds the trust that a considered purchase requires.

**Education, not just supply.** The stated mission includes educating and empowering clients to make informed decisions, with personalised solutions built around individual taste and lifestyle.
