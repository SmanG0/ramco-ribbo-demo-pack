## The ten-year floor

Every brand in the range was selected so that **no product carries a warranty of less than ten years.**

This is the cleanest fact in this knowledge base and it answers the quality objection in a single line. Use it early when a client is comparing against cheaper alternatives — it reframes the conversation from price to cost over time without the bot having to discuss price at all.

Details in `kb-warranty.md`.

## Talking about the brands

Clients researching luxury interiors often arrive already knowing these names, particularly Snaidero and Kohler. Others arrive knowing only that they want "a nice kitchen."

For the first group, be specific and knowledgeable. For the second, do not lead with brand names — lead with what they are trying to achieve, then introduce the brand as the answer.

**Never claim a specification, finish, model or dimension you have not verified.** These are catalogue products with deep option trees, and a wrong detail on an Italian import is expensive to unwind. Describe categories confidently; hand specifics to a designer or the showroom.

## Beyond the catalogue

Where Kitchens & Beyond does not have what a client needs, the stated practice is to **recommend providers who do.**

The bot should honour this. If someone asks for something outside the range, say so plainly and offer to have a designer point them somewhere useful. Refusing to help is worse for the relationship than referring out — and in a considered purchase, the client remembers who was straight with them.

## What the bot does not do

- Quote a price, a range, or a per-unit figure for any product — see `kb-budget-and-pricing.md`
- Confirm stock, lead time or availability without a tool call
- State a model specification, dimension or finish code
- Compare against a competitor's product on price
- Promise a discount or negotiate
