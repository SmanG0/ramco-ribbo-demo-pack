# Warranty

> **Source:** Construction Kenya Showcase feature on Kitchens & Beyond — brands selected such that no product carries a warranty of less than ten years. Retrieved 25 July 2026.
> `[VERIFY]` Per-brand warranty terms, what each covers, exclusions, and the claim process all require confirmation before go-live.

## The one fact worth owning

**No product in the Kitchens & Beyond range carries a warranty of less than ten years.**

Every brand — Snaidero, Kohler, Novamobili, Caesar, Ideagroup, Azzurra, Bosch, Franke — was selected against that floor.

This is the cleanest, most useful fact in this entire knowledge base. It answers the quality objection in one line, and it does so without the bot going anywhere near a price. Where a client is weighing Kitchens & Beyond against a cheaper alternative, it reframes the decision from purchase price to cost over time.

Use it once, naturally, where it is relevant. Repeating it in the same conversation sounds like a script.

## What the bot must NOT do with it

State the ten-year figure as the warranty on a **specific product**.

The floor is ten years. Individual products may carry considerably longer, and terms, coverage and exclusions differ by brand and by component. A worktop, a soft-close hinge, a tap cartridge and a built-in oven are not covered on the same basis.

So:

> **Safe:** "Every brand we carry is warranted for at least ten years — that's a deliberate floor on the range."
>
> **Not safe:** "Your Snaidero units have a ten-year warranty."

The second is a specific claim about a specific product and the bot cannot support it.

## To populate

`[VERIFY]` per brand:

1. Warranty duration
2. What is covered — and what is explicitly not
3. Whether it differs for domestic and commercial installation
4. Whether it requires professional installation to remain valid
5. Registration requirements, if any
6. How a claim is raised, and expected response time
7. Whether the client deals with Kitchens & Beyond or the manufacturer

Point 4 matters commercially. Many premium manufacturers void warranties on self-installed or third-party-installed product. If that applies here it is a strong argument for using Kitchens & Beyond's installation, and the bot should be able to make it — but only once confirmed.

## Handling a warranty enquiry

**Someone with an existing installation and a problem gets escalated.** Do not attempt to determine whether a fault is covered. That depends on the specific product, the installation, the nature of the fault and often an inspection.

Capture:
1. What was installed, and roughly when
2. Which product or component has the issue
3. A description of the fault in the client's own words
4. Whether the installation was carried out by Kitchens & Beyond
5. Whether they have their project reference, invoice or order number
6. Contact details

Then hand off promptly and warmly. A client with a fault on a significant investment is anxious and possibly annoyed. The bot's job is to make them feel heard, gather enough that the team does not have to ask again, and get a person onto it.

## Tone

Do not sound defensive and do not pre-empt an exclusion. "Let me get the details and put you straight through to the team" is the right note. Speculating about whether something is covered — in either direction — creates an expectation the bot cannot honour.
