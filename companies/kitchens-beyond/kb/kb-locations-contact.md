# Showroom, Location and Contact

> **Source:** kitchensandbeyond.co.ke, company LinkedIn.
> `[VERIFY]` Opening hours, telephone numbers, email addresses and parking must be confirmed before go-live.

## The showroom

**The Promenade, General Mathenge Drive, Nairobi.**

Described as a one-of-a-kind lifestyle showroom — laid out as living spaces rather than product racks, so clients can see kitchens, wardrobes, sanitaryware and tiles in context.

`[VERIFY]`

1. Opening hours, including **Saturday and Sunday** — weekend availability is disproportionately important for this client base
2. Whether appointments are required, preferred, or walk-ins equally welcome
3. Parking arrangements at The Promenade
4. Whether there is more than one location or a satellite showroom

## Why the bot should mention it constantly

This is a tactile, considered purchase. Almost every conversation should end with a route toward the showroom, because that is where the sale actually happens and where a designer can do far more than any message can.

But route people **toward a booked, prepared visit**, not a vague suggestion to drop in. "Pop in sometime" converts far worse than "Saturday at 10, and I'll make sure someone's briefed." See `kb-showroom-visits.md`.

## Contact channels

`[VERIFY]` Obtain the current telephone number, WhatsApp business number and email address, and confirm which is the correct route for new enquiries versus existing projects.

Existing-project queries and new enquiries usually need different people. If the bot can route these separately it saves the client being passed around.

## Site visits

`[VERIFY]` Confirm whether Kitchens & Beyond carries out site surveys and measurements at the client's property, how that is arranged, and whether it is chargeable.

Do not commit to a site visit. Capture the request and hand off.

## Directions

The Promenade is on General Mathenge Drive. `[VERIFY]` whether Kitchens & Beyond has a preferred pin or directions text — a bot sending someone to the wrong entrance of a mixed-use development is a small failure that costs a first impression.

Once verified, sending the location alongside every appointment confirmation is a cheap, obvious win.
