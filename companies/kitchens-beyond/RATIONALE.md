# Why Kitchens & Beyond — and why NOT Kentainers

Issued 25 July 2026.

## The Kentainers problem

`03-ramco-printing.md` and the Ramco Group Target Map both recommended **Kentainers** as the optional fourth demo, listed under Ramco Altus.

**Do not build it. Ramco no longer owns Kentainers.**

In June 2023 Vantage Capital invested USD 25m into Aquasantec in a leveraged management buy-out, acquiring a controlling shareholding. The founding Shah family, **Ramco Group** and Terra Mauricia all **disposed of their shareholding** as part of that transaction. Source: Kentainers' own press release page, kentainers.co.ke/projects/press-release.

Both kentainers.co.ke and ramco-group.com still describe it as a Ramco joint venture. Both pages are stale — the Ramco page dates from 2015.

**Two corrections required:**

1. Remove Kentainers from the Altus vertical in the Target Map HTML
2. Remove the Kentainers recommendation from section 3 of `03-ramco-printing.md`

Ramco's own current company listing never included Kentainers under Altus. It was added in error during target-map drafting.

Pitching a family-run conglomerate a demo built on a business they exited three years ago would undo the credibility the other three packages buy. Worth catching.

## Why Kitchens & Beyond

**Verified current.** Listed on ramco-group.com under Ekon (Building Materials), live site at kitchensandbeyond.co.ke.

### 1. A fourth genuinely distinct agent pattern

The four demos now cover four different shapes, which is the entire platform argument:

| Company | Vertical | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Sai Office | Kora | Lookup and transact |
| Ramco Printing | Plexus | Structured capture |
| Safarilink | Oritsu | Inventory query + identity verification |
| **Kitchens & Beyond** | **Ekon** | **Consultative qualification + appointment booking** |

Nothing else in the demo **books** anything. This bot's write action is a calendar slot, not an order.

### 2. It is the only one that makes money rather than saving it

The other three are cost and deflection arguments — queries absorbed, staff time freed, jobs unblocked. Kitchens & Beyond is a **revenue** argument.

A luxury interiors purchase is considered, researched at night, and highly leakable. Someone browsing Italian kitchens at 11pm either gets engaged or goes to a competitor by morning. A bot that qualifies them, captures the brief and books the showroom appointment converts leakage into pipeline.

**Put this bot last in the demo.** Three bots that save money, then one that makes money. That is the note to end on.

### 3. Fourth vertical, and Ekon is the heritage business

Ramco began in 1948 as a Nairobi hardware business — Ekon is where the group started. Covering Kora, Plexus, Oritsu and Ekon means four of six verticals, which makes the group-platform argument concrete rather than theoretical.

### 4. Strong credentials to work with

- **Superbrands status 2021–22, awarded by public vote**
- 13+ years trading
- Brand roster: Snaidero (Italy), Kohler, Novamobili, Caesar, Ideagroup, Azzurra, Bosch, Franke
- **No product in the range carries a warranty of less than ten years**
- In-house interior design and **3D visualisation**
- Lifestyle showroom, The Promenade, General Mathenge Drive

That ten-year warranty floor is an unusually clean fact for a bot to own — it answers the quality objection in one line.

## The unifying pitch line this unlocks

Three of the four bots now surface the same planted problem from completely different businesses:

- **Ramco Printing** — a job waiting on the customer's proof approval
- **Sai Office** — a repair finished and waiting on collection, and a quote waiting on a yes
- **Kitchens & Beyond** — an installation waiting on the client's tile selection

> In every one of your businesses, work is sitting still waiting on your own customers, and nobody is telling them.

That is a group-level insight, not four product demos. It is the argument for a group platform agreement, and it is worth building the close around.

## Alternatives considered

**Multivista** — sits under Oritsu, already covered by Safarilink. Deep industrial domain would demo shallow.
**ASL Engineering** (Altus) — dairy plant equipment. Genuinely interesting sector, but the technical depth needs real product data to avoid looking thin.
**ASL Trading** (Ekon) — enormous unstructured hardware catalogue. Strong eventual fit, poor demo; the build would be all data cleaning.
**Domus** — leadership has publicly said they would rather separate property from the operating businesses. Not a customer.
